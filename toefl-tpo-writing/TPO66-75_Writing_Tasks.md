## TPO 75 — Writing

### Task 1 · Integrated Writing
**Topic:** Controlling the invasive Northern Pacific sea star (*Asterias amurensis*) — 控制入侵性北太平洋海星

**Reading passage (summary):** The Northern Pacific sea star is native to the coasts of Japan, Korea, and China but has recently appeared thousands of kilometers away, where it threatens marine ecosystems. The reading proposes three strategies to control its spread: (1) administer a poison called **quicklime** (calcium oxide), spread in powdered form over invaded seafloor — said to destroy sea stars without harming commercially important mollusks such as oysters, scallops, and mussels; (2) introduce **natural parasites** that specifically attack the sea star; and (3) **sterilize the ballast water** of ships so larvae are not transported to new regions.

**Lecture (summary):** The professor argues none of the three strategies would work. (1) Quicklime would also kill *native* sea stars, which help maintain local ecosystems, and it stays active on the seafloor long enough that native sea stars re-entering treated areas would also die. (2) A parasite strong enough to control the invaders would likely also attack native sea star species and may not be target-specific. (3) Sterilizing ballast water does nothing about populations already established, and the invaders spread by other means too — so it cannot stop the ongoing spread.

**Prompt (verbatim):**
> Summarize the points made in the lecture, being sure to explain how they challenge the specific arguments presented in the reading passage.
